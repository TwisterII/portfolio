BOSS FIGHTS

  1. BOSS 1

    Online Check-in & Checkout: the legacy arrival tool

    Stale, project-managed, no analytics

    THE PROBLEM

    Arrival and departure ran on a legacy system led by project managers who saw it as a tool. It had no analytics, so nobody could say what made a guest experience better.

    THE CALL

    Treat arrival as a product, not a project. I instrumented it first, then shipped weekly in response to guest behavior and the changing business, on one platform with property-configurable pricing and Front Desk internal controls for operators.

    THE RESULT

    Guests can now skip the front desk entirely, if they choose.

    Monthly upsell revenue has doubled (Jan → Jun 2026)

    • $2M+upsell revenue in 2026
    • WEEKLYcode releases
  2. BOSS 2

    Digital Key: scaling safely

    Expansion was outpacing its safeguards

    THE PROBLEM

    Digital Key and kiosk expansion was moving fast, and a production issue exposed gaps in monitoring and testing.

    THE CALL

    We stepped back to build two kinds of confidence: pre-release (new regression testing) and operational (observability, alerting, runbooks). I separated software-release readiness from property rollout and set a risk-based roadmap to resume expansion.

    THE RESULT

    Less guest and agent friction, more revenue, and renewed stakeholder confidence.

    Mobile-app Digital Key adoption 9.1% → 14% (Jun → Sep 2026)

    • +15%uptime, last 2 months
    • 22K+keys issued through Sep 2026
    • 5%reduction in front-desk traffic, with no Digital Key marketing

    More front-desk reduction is expected; it becomes measurable in 2027.
